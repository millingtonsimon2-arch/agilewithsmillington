A little about me

More than a framework. More than a job title.

I'm an Agile Coach with a broad professional background spanning delivery management, project and programme management, product, business analysis, engineering and organisational change.

Working across different industries, roles and delivery environments has shaped my approach. I understand that organisations are complex, people have different perspectives and there is rarely a single answer to improving how work gets done.

I'm interested in what makes teams and organisations effective: how people collaborate, make decisions, manage work, respond to feedback and deliver value.

My approach is pragmatic and people-focused, drawing on Agile and Lean principles, practical delivery experience and a belief in continuous learning.

My philosophy

Good ways of working aren't created by blindly following a process or adopting another framework.

They come from understanding the challenges, creating the right conditions for people to succeed and finding practical ways to improve.

Sometimes that means challenging assumptions. Sometimes it's about asking a better question. And sometimes it's about trying something completely different.

A pragmatic mindset

Less buzzword bingo. More meaningful improvement.

Agile is something you do, not just something you say. I'm interested in understanding what is really happening and helping people find approaches that work.

Understand the system

Look beyond individual teams and processes. Understand relationships, constraints, dependencies and the wider environment in which people work.

Make improvement practical

Use appropriate tools and techniques, challenge assumptions and focus on changes that make a meaningful difference.

Learn by doing

Encourage people to experiment, reflect, adapt and build their own understanding through experience rather than theory alone.

A different way to learn

Welcome to Agile Arcade

Small games. Big ideas.

What if learning Agile and Lean didn't mean sitting through another presentation or memorising another set of principles?

Agile Arcade is my growing collection of interactive games, challenges and experiments designed to bring Agile and Lean concepts to life.

Play around, experiment, make mistakes and discover what happens. The aim is to create those lightbulb moments that make learning memorable and useful.

PLAY → REFLECT → LEARN → APPLY

🔀

Multitasking Mayhem

Discover how switching between tasks affects focus, efficiency and the flow of work.

👾

The WIP Monster

Explore work in progress, bottlenecks, testing and the challenges of too much work at once.

🎯

Priority Shuffle

Explore prioritisation, competing demands and making decisions about value.

🐞

Bug Bash

Discover the importance of quality, collaboration and finding problems early.

🧩

Slice It Small

Explore smaller batches, incremental delivery and getting feedback sooner.

💬

Feedback Frenzy

Explore feedback loops, learning and the value of sharing information early.

No lengthy manuals. No complicated theory. Just practical learning through play.
